# Stream K: kelp and seagrass

Package `kelp`. Config `kelp` (`enabled`, `generate`). Built in the first session.

## Changelog section

*   **Kelp**: place it on the sea floor; it grows up toward the surface, a little at a time, and stops at
    the top of the water. Bone meal grows it a block; shears on the top stop it growing. Breaking any piece
    breaks everything above it. Every piece drops one kelp.
*   **Dried kelp**: smelt kelp. Restores 1 hunger and is eaten twice as fast as other food. Nine make a
    **dried kelp block**, which burns for 20 items in a furnace.
*   **Seagrass** and **tall seagrass** grow on sea, river and swamp floors. Collect seagrass with shears.
    Bone meal on seagrass makes it tall; bone meal on the sea floor spreads seagrass around it (and coral, in
    warm oceans).
*   Kelp forests and seagrass meadows generate in newly explored oceans, rivers and swamps: kelp in every
    ocean but warm and frozen ones.
*   With a composter installed, kelp, dried kelp and seagrass compost at 30% and the dried kelp block at 50%.

## How it works

*   **No waterlogging needed**: kelp and seagrass are `common/BlockPseudoWater` blocks. They *are* the water
    in their space: water material and vanilla water's `level` property fixed at 0, so swimming, drowning,
    fog, boats, fish, flow and source forming all treat them as still water. Breaking leaves water. At the
    surface the model adds a biome-tinted water top. A bucket takes nothing from them.
*   **Age in 16 steps**: 1.13's kelp top has ages 0-25; 1.12.2 has 16 metadata values. The top's age runs
    0-15 and a new top takes the next age 15 times in 25, which keeps the expected height of a planted kelp
    the same as 1.13's.
*   **Shears on seagrass** are handled in `harvestBlock`, not with Forge's `IShearable` (vanilla's shears
    would leave air instead of water).
*   **Numbers** from 1.14.4: growth 14% per random tick; dried kelp food 1 / saturation 0.3 / 16 ticks;
    dried kelp block hardness 0.5, blast resistance 2.5, fuel 4001 ticks, flammability 30/60; generation
    counts per ocean temperature (see `KelpGenerator`). Today's additions: bone meal on kelp (1.16), shears
    stopping kelp (1.18), seagrass in warm oceans.

## Tested headlessly

`./gradlew runServer -Pselftest=kelp`, then `python tools/check_assets.py`: **`SELFTEST DONE pass=687 fail=0`**
(with the woodset test in the same run), **34 blocks, 1360 states, 33 item models, 34 sound files, 0
errors**.

*   Registration of the five blocks and four items; no item for kelp plant and tall seagrass; every stored
    state survives a metadata round trip; all are water material.
*   Placing by a real player's right-click: on the floor under water (top with a starting age below 15,
    water level 0), not on dry land, not on magma.
*   Water to everything: a pig in kelp is in water; a player's eyes in kelp are under water; a boat over a
    kelp column ticks 20 times without error (vanilla reads water level there); an empty bucket on kelp is
    refused and the kelp stays.
*   Growth to exactly the highest water block, top over plant, the water surface shown on the top only; a
    fully grown top does not grow; 86% of ticks nothing happens; bone meal grows a fully grown top and grows
    from a plant piece, using one bone meal; shears set full growth and wear.
*   Breaking the bottom leaves water, the column above breaks bottom-up and drops one kelp per piece; an
    explosion leaves water; flowing water beside kelp leaves it alone.
*   Seagrass: placed under water, nothing by hand, itself with shears, water left behind; bone meal makes
    both halves of tall seagrass; shears on tall seagrass drop two and remove both halves; a block placed into
    seagrass replaces it; a block placed at kelp does not.
*   Underwater bone meal on the floor uses one and spreads at least 8 seagrass; on dry stone does nothing.
*   Dried kelp: smelting with 0.1 experience, food values and eating time, 9 to a block and back, fuel 4001,
    hardness 0.5, blast resistance 2.5.
*   Generation: the kelp feature in a pool grows columns that never reach the air; a real ocean found near
    spawn and generated by the world generator had **3285 kelp and 1059 seagrass blocks in 25 chunks**.
*   The server booting at all proves no client type is reached from server code.
