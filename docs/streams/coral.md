# Stream C: coral

Package `coral`. Config `coral` (`enabled`, `generate`). Built in the first session.

## Changelog section

*   **Coral blocks** in tube, brain, bubble, fire and horn, and their **dead** forms. A coral block with no
    water touching it dies a few seconds later. Mined with a pickaxe it drops its dead form; with Silk Touch,
    itself.
*   **Coral** and **coral fans** in the same five, living and dead. Fans go on the floor or on the side of a
    block. Placed in water they are waterlogged; out of water (and not touching any) they die. A bucket
    takes their water or gives it back. They drop only with Silk Touch.
*   **Coral reefs** in warm oceans: coral trees, claws and mushrooms, with coral, fans and sea pickles growing
    on them.
*   **Bone meal on a sea pickle on a coral block** makes it four pickles and spreads more over the coral
    around.
*   Bone meal on the floor of a warm ocean grows coral and fans among the seagrass.

## How it works

*   Coral and fans are `common/BlockPseudoWater` blocks with 1.13's `waterlogged` property: waterlogged, they
    are the water there (as kelp is); dry, they are a material that blocks flowing water, so water never
    washes them away (in 1.13 flowing water cannot enter a waterloggable block either).
*   Life rule from 1.14.4: a living coral plant or fan lives while waterlogged *or* touching water on any
    face; a coral block while touching water. Checks run on placement and on every neighbor change; death
    comes 60-99 ticks later if still dry.
*   Wall fans are separate blocks (`<type>_coral_wall_fan`) placed by the fan item against a side, as 1.13's
    wall-or-floor item does; they drop and pick as the fan.
*   Reefs are 1.14.4's three coral features, with its density (`ceil(noise(x/400, z/400) * 20)` per chunk) and
    the warm ocean's sea pickle patches (1 chunk in 16, 20 tries). Warm comes from `common/OceanKind`.
*   Sea pickles in reefs and the bone meal spreading use whichever sea pickle exists (the owner's or this
    mod's copy), matched by registry name and property names.

## Tested headlessly

`./gradlew runServer -Pselftest=all`, then `python tools/check_assets.py`: **`SELFTEST DONE pass=867 fail=1`**
across all four tests, the one failure a test-site choice since fixed (coral alone afterwards:
**`pass=171 fail=0`**); **74 blocks, 5210 states, 63 item models, 60 sound files, 0 errors**.

*   40 blocks and 30 items; every stored state survives a metadata round trip; a wall fan picks as its fan.
*   Coral blocks: placed on land, checked and dead within the delay; alive beside one enclosed water block,
    dead once it is removed; hardness 1.5, blast resistance 6, pickaxe; mined drops the dead block, Silk
    Touch drops itself.
*   Coral and fans: waterlogged when placed in water (and water material), alive; placed on land dry, not
    water, blocking flow, dead within the delay and dry; a dry coral touching water lives; a water bucket
    waterlogs a dry one (bucket comes back empty), an empty bucket drains it (comes back full); nothing
    dropped without Silk Touch, itself with Silk Touch on any tool; breaking leaves water; losing support
    removes it, leaving water and no drop.
*   Wall fans: a fan placed on a side becomes a wall fan facing out, waterlogged; on top, a floor fan; they
    rotate; Silk Touch drops the fan item; they go with their support; a dry one dies keeping its facing.
*   Sea pickles on coral: bone meal makes four and spreads pickles over all 12 water-over-coral spots of the
    diamond (with chance forced to 1); a real bone meal use on a pickle on coral is consumed.
*   Reef shapes in a deep pool build at least 10 coral blocks; a real warm ocean generated by the world
    generator had **4513 coral blocks in 25 chunks**, a cold one **0**.
