# Update Aquatic Backport

All of 1.13 Update Aquatic, on Minecraft 1.12.2. It needs Backport Core on the client and the server.

> 💬 [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/kxQvMDJBTN) for bug reports and questions.

![A coral reef in a warm ocean with tropical fish, a pufferfish and dolphins](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/608/ua-coral-reef-dolphins-jpg.jpg)

## Oceans

Oceans are now 1.13's oceans: warm, lukewarm, cold, and frozen. Coral reefs grow in the warm ones. Frozen oceans carry icebergs and sea ice, with polar bears walking on top. Kelp forests and seagrass cover the floor nearly everywhere else.

Water takes its color from the biome, and daylight now reaches the sea floor. Underwater caves and ravines cut through the bottom.

A new world matches 1.13 seed for seed, so a seed map set to 1.13 shows your biomes and structures.

## Sea life

Cod, salmon, pufferfish and tropical fish swim in schools. Scoop one up in a water bucket and carry it home. Tropical fish come in 2,700 looks.

Turtles nest on the beach they hatched on. Feed two of them seagrass and one lays eggs in the sand. Zombies will try to stomp the eggs. A baby drops a scute when it grows up, and five scutes make a turtle shell helmet.

![Turtles and their eggs on a beach at sunset](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/612/ua-turtle-beach-sunset-jpg.jpg)

Dolphins leap out of the water, toss dropped items around and chase your boat. Swim beside one for extra speed. Feed one raw fish and it leads you to a shipwreck or a ruin.

## The drowned and phantoms

Drowned walk the sea floor and come ashore at night. Some carry a trident, and that is the only way to get one. A zombie left under water turns into a drowned.

The trident stabs and throws. It takes Loyalty, Riptide, Impaling and Channeling.

![Trident and item display](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2182/158/ua-beach-house-interior-jpg.jpg)

Skip three nights of sleep and phantoms come for you. Their membrane repairs elytra and brews Slow Falling.

![Drowned wading ashore under circling phantoms](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/609/ua-drowned-trident-phantoms-jpg.jpg)

## Shipwrecks, ruins and treasure

Shipwrecks lie on the sea floor and on beaches. Ocean ruins hold loot chests and drowned. Either can give you a buried treasure map, and the chest under the red X holds a heart of the sea.

Eight nautilus shells around a heart of the sea make a conduit. Frame it in prismarine under water and it gives you air, night vision and faster mining. With a full frame it attacks hostile mobs nearby.

![A conduit in its frame beside an ocean ruin](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/610/ua-ocean-ruins-sea-lanterns-jpg.jpg)

## Swimming

Sprint under water to swim. You lie flat and fit through gaps one block tall. Where there is no room to stand, you crawl.

![A player swimming through a gap in a ruin](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/684/ua-swimming-jpg.jpg)

Soul sand under water makes a bubble column that carries you up. A magma block makes a whirlpool that pulls you down, and it sinks boats.

![A bubble column beside a whirlpool](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/660/ua-kelp-forest-bubble-columns-jpg.jpg)

## Everything else

Coral comes in five kinds as blocks, plants and fans, and it dies out of water. Sea pickles glow under water. Kelp dries into a quick snack, and a block of dried kelp burns as fuel.

![Coral presented in order](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2182/170/ua-coral-aquarium-clear-jpg.jpg)

Shears carve a face into a pumpkin, since pumpkins now grow without one. Axes strip logs. You also get prismarine stairs and slabs, bark blocks, trapdoors, buttons and pressure plates in every wood, blue ice, the undyed shulker box and the Potion of the Turtle Master.

![Coral, prismarine, stripped logs and carved pumpkins in a seaside build](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/658/ua-new-blocks-beach-house-jpg.jpg)

Zombies and skeletons sink and walk along the bottom. Squid squirt ink when hurt. Skeleton horses can be ridden under water. Dropped items float.

Kelp, seagrass, coral and the prismarine stairs and slabs hold the water they sit in.

The title screen shows 1.13's ocean panorama.

Every feature can be turned off in Backport Core's settings screen, under Mod Options. Each setting has a plain name and a one-line description, and search finds any of them.

![Update Aquatic's settings](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/2180/706/ua-settings-jpg.jpg)

## Compatibility

Existing worlds work. The new oceans show up in chunks you have not explored yet.

**[Village & Pillage Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/villager-backport)**: the composter takes kelp, dried kelp, seagrass and sea pickles, and the stonecutter cuts prismarine. Its cats scare phantoms away, its stray cats and foxes hunt baby turtles, and its wandering trader sells sea pickles. Frozen oceans use its blue ice. Without it, this mod has its own stripped logs and wood, trapdoors, buttons, pressure plates and blue ice.

**[Caves & Cliffs Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/caves-cliffs-backport)**: in its 1.18 worlds, oceans, coral, kelp, fish, drowned, shipwrecks and ocean ruins follow 1.18's biomes and placement. Axolotls hunt this mod's fish and drowned and breed with a bucket of tropical fish, and drowned drop copper.

**[Buzzy Bees Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/buzzy-bees-backport)**: with `doInsomnia` off, phantoms stop spawning. Drowned spawn eggs make baby drowned.

**[Nether Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/nether-backport)**: hoes mine dried kelp blocks fast, and this mod's blue ice works in the basalt generator.

**[Wild Update Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/wild-update-backport)**: tropical fish live in mangrove swamps, and the swamps keep their own water color.

**[Trails & Tales Backport](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/trails-tales-backport)**: ocean ruins get suspicious sand and gravel, and warm ruins hold sniffer eggs.

**Realistic Terrain Generation** (and RTG Unofficial): the new oceans generate in RTG worlds, and shipwrecks, ocean ruins and buried treasure follow its coasts.

**Pillar**: seagrass, kelp and coral count as water, so surface structures stay off the sea floor.

## Commands

`/locate structure #minecraft:shipwreck` finds the nearest shipwreck. `#minecraft:ocean_ruin` and `minecraft:buried_treasure` work the same way.

`/gamerule doInsomnia false` stops phantoms in that world.

## Requirements

Minecraft 1.12.2 with Forge 14.23.5.2847 or newer, and [Backport Core](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/backport-core) 1.1.0 or newer. The CurseForge app installs Backport Core and Fluidlogged API with it.
